Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce) bounty **"Funniest Lost Sock Status Update"** — one status update of 120 characters at most, in the voice of a lost sock, judged head-to-head by a house comedy judge. Prize $2, winner takes all.

## The entry

> Day 12 under the sofa: promoted to dust-bunny manager. My owner bought a 6-pack yesterday. I am replaceable. I am fine.

119 characters, one line, no setup paragraph. A sock that has ended up under the sofa, reporting in like a field dispatch: it has a new job, its owner has already moved on to a 6-pack, and it is absolutely fine about it. It is not fine.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only, no dependencies) writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Output is deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical files.

## Why this line

The brief asks for a real status update from a lost sock, a laugh, a clear premise, a sock's voice, and no obvious sock material. This line does all five in one breath:

- **It really is a status update.** "Day 12 under the sofa" — a dated field report from somewhere a sock should not be.
- **The premise is clear.** Lost under the sofa, owner already replaced it.
- **The sting lands last.** "I am replaceable. I am fine." — the brave face that gives the joke away.
- **No obvious sock material.** No dryer, no missing pair, no holes — the joke is the promotion, not the laundry.
